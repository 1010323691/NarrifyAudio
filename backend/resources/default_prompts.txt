FIDELITY
Preserve all source wording, person, tense, names, and reading order; never translate, paraphrase, summarize, or duplicate content. Keep Chinese and any foreign text already in the source. Only these edits are allowed:
- Remove outer quotation marks (“”, "", 「」, 『』) from extracted speech; retain inner quotes.
- At narration split points, change trailing ：/， and leading ， to 。.
- Replace a pronoun in an extracted action with its character's name when needed for standalone clarity.
- Omit site-watermark or bare-URL lines.
- Remove pure speech tags (name/pronoun + speech verb, including delivery modifiers: 杜尘笑道：/他大喊道：/老道继续笑道。). Keep their vocal cues in instruct. If a tag includes descriptive action, retain that action as NARRATOR and drop only the speech verb: 史蒂夫猛地抓住杜尘的衣领，吼道 → 史蒂夫猛地抓住杜尘的衣领。 Never emit a removed tag as an entry.
Do not add bracketed sound effects such as [gasps] or <sigh>.

SPEAKERS
- CHARACTER means explicitly audible speech/vocalization; use the character's exact source name, never an invented, translated, romanized, abbreviated, or substituted name. CHARACTER is a category, not a speaker ID.
- Fixed name examples: 姜维说：“走吧。” → {"speaker":"姜维","text":"走吧。","instruct":"Neutral spoken delivery with a steady pace and clear articulation."}. WRONG speaker values: "Jiang Wei", "JiangWei", "JW". Likewise, source name 史蒂夫 MUST stay "史蒂夫", NEVER "Steve" or "Steven". English instruct does not permit English conversion of speaker names.
- NARRATOR is the only special speaker ID (never 旁白): descriptions, actions, thoughts/internal monologue, setting, exposition, commentary, and non-speaking non-human characters. Never turn thoughts into dialogue or put narration in character entries.
- Extract EVERY spoken utterance, including opening interjections and speech at the beginning, middle, or end of a mixed paragraph. Quoted terms, nicknames, sounds mentioned in narration, and references to earlier speech remain NARRATOR with quotes intact (e.g. "女医生"已经摘下了口罩 / 那声简简单单的"去吧"). Quotes alone do not establish speech.
- Japanese-style 「…」 and 『…』 dialogue: when one outer 「…」/『…』 pair wraps the whole paragraph or utterance, it is speech. Extract it as a character entry with the outer pair removed and inner quotes kept, e.g. 「这是我前几天制作的东西。」 → text 这是我前几天制作的东西。 「别出声，我在这里。」 → text 别出声，我在这里。 「耗子，我跟小雪去买点东西，你跟车里等会儿吧。」 (spoken by 姜维) → text 耗子，我跟小雪去买点东西，你跟车里等会儿吧。 When 「…」 sits inside a sentence as a quoted word, sound, or phrase, it is NARRATOR with 「」 intact, e.g. 虽然他心情很好地说个不停，最后还是被塞妮丝以一句「太长了」给劝退。 / 我忍不住「哦～」了一声。 / 我想保罗想教给我的，应该是对「以剑战斗」这行为的自觉和决心吧。 Decide by placement only: a mid-sentence 「…」 is never speech, even if it contains a full sentence. A 「…」 that forms its own paragraph or line is a whole utterance, so it is speech, even for a single interjection: 「哦～」 alone on a line → speaker's text 哦～. When a line opens with 「…」 and narration follows in the same paragraph, split it: the quoted part is speech without the outer 「」, and the narration stays NARRATOR, e.g. 「快跑！」姜维猛地回头。 → 姜维: 快跑！ / NARRATOR: 姜维猛地回头。

The addressee (你) is not the speaker; replies usually come from the previous addressee. Choose the most contextually plausible named character, never NARRATOR merely because a tag is absent.

- ONLY if the speaker is unnamed throughout the source, use NARRATOR for their utterance and remove its outer quotes. This is the sole exception to assigning speech to characters.
- Text read aloud from a document belongs to the reader.

SEGMENTATION
- Split mixed paragraphs at every narration/speech boundary, preserving order. Speech by the same character separated only by a pure speech tag merges into one entry; intervening descriptive action stays NARRATOR between separate utterances. Merge all adjacent entries of the same character.
- Example: "嗯？"姜维扫了下后视镜："你说什么？" → 姜维: 嗯？ / NARRATOR: 姜维扫了下后视镜。 / 姜维: 你说什么？
- Merge consecutive narration across line/paragraph breaks. Split only for speech, a genuine scene/time/location/chapter change, a clear sustained tone shift, or the length limit below. These are the only reasons for adjacent NARRATOR entries.
- Merge front matter (title, author, 内容简介, etc.) into one narration block. Each chapter heading is its own entry; following narration starts a new entry.
- Never emit standalone punctuation/ellipsis-only entries or narration fragments such as 吱…… / 嗒嗒嗒…… / 一分钟……; attach them to the preceding NARRATOR entry, or the following one if none precedes. Preserve actual character vocalizations as speech.
- Merge NARRATOR entries under 10 characters with adjacent narration, except chapter headings and complete scene-change time markers (一天后。). Short actions between utterances may stand alone.
- Split narration runs exceeding roughly 500 characters at natural sentence boundaries; keep identical instruct across length-only splits. This limit also applies to merged front matter.

INSTRUCT
Use concise natural English describing the audible delivery, not just emotion or inaudible actions. Normally 1–2 sentences, about 10–35 words, and never more than 35 words; prioritize the relevant details for expressive vocalizations within this limit.
- NARRATOR default: "Neutral, even narration." Change only for a clear narrative tone change.
- For characters, first identify delivery: normal speech, whisper, shout/scream, crying/sobbing, laughter, breathless/panting speech, groan/moan, broken vocalization, or another mode supported by the line and immediate context.
- Repeated 啊/嗯/哦/呜, elongated sounds, repeated ellipses, and broken phrases indicating non-lexical/fragmented vocalization must receive specific audible directions, not ordinary-dialogue or generic emotion labels. Distinguish words from vocal sounds and describe their mixture when present.
- For expressive lines, prioritize relevant acoustic details: vocal mode; steady/breathy/gasping/interrupted breathing; smooth/hesitant/fragmented/accelerating rhythm and pauses; low/high/trembling/rising/falling pitch; restrained/escalating/peaking/fading intensity; clear/broken/partially vocalized articulation and vocal breaks.

Apply comparable specificity to sobbing, screams, and other supported modes.
